About Me

I am a passionate Mehandi artist specializing in bridal, Arabic and customized designs. My goal is to create beautiful and detailed designs that make your special occasions unforgettable.

With years of experience and a deep love for the art of henna, I bring creativity, precision, and elegance to every design. Each pattern is crafted with care to reflect your unique style and personality.

My Services

Specializing in various Mehandi styles for all occasions

Bridal Mehandi

Intricate and traditional bridal designs that add elegance to your wedding day. Full hand and feet coverage with personalized elements.

Arabic Mehandi

Bold and beautiful Arabic patterns featuring flowing vines, floral motifs, and contemporary designs perfect for any celebration.

Engagement / Party Mehandi

Stylish and trendy designs perfect for engagements, sangeet, and other pre-wedding functions. Modern patterns with a traditional touch.

Festival Mehandi

Beautiful designs for festivals like Diwali, Eid, Karva Chauth, and more. Traditional motifs celebrating cultural heritage.

Kids Mehandi

Fun and simple designs for children featuring cute patterns, cartoon characters, and playful motifs that kids will love.
